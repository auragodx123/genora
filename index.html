About GENORA

Simple tools for getting unstuck and moving ideas forward.

GENORA is a free collection of lightweight online generators designed to help people brainstorm names and creative ideas. Whether you are starting a gaming profile, planning a team, naming a channel or exploring a business idea, GENORA helps you create a starting point quickly.

Our tools are intended for inspiration. Review and check any generated name before using it publicly, especially for business names, trademarks, usernames and domain availability.
